#  Price Comparison System

##  Problem Statement
Customers are required to browse multiple e-commerce platforms to compare product prices, which is time-consuming and inefficient.  
The absence of a unified system for real-time comparison often results in poor purchasing decisions.



##  Core Issues
- Lack of a single platform to compare prices across multiple e-commerce websites  
- Time-consuming manual process of visiting different platforms  
- Difficulty in making informed decisions due to scattered and non-uniform information  



##  User Challenges
- Users face difficulty in searching for products when exact names or descriptions are unknown  
- Traditional text-based search is not effective for visually unique or unfamiliar products  



## Existing System Limitations
- Existing platforms provide cluttered information with multiple sellers and options  
- Lack of simple and focused comparison between selected platforms  
- Absence of image-based search functionality for better user experience
